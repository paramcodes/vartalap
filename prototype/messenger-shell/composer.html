ADR behind each choice

  • One surface, attach inside, send outside. 36px circles; attach is icon-only with no fill until hover; send is accent with a white glyph. The surface grows to 160px then scrolls internally.
  • Enter to send needs three guards, and no one is sufficient. A flag we own from onCompositionStart/onCompositionEnd, cleared in a queueMicrotask — that microtask is what survives Safari's ordering — plus the property, plus a key-event guard. This is not a solved problem; it is three separate ways a browser gets IME composition wrong. Use IME in the top bar to see Enter decline to send.
  • Media stages before the message exists. Pressing send sends the text and every READY attachment and removes nothing from the tray. Blocking send greys out the button while a 12MB photo uploads over hotel wifi; silently dropping unfinished attachments is the exact failure the durable spine exists to exclude.
  • The draft clears on queue-entry, not on send. Once a message is queued it owns its text; the field is free for the next one.
  • A 409 is terminal and says so. A reused idempotency key with different content is a buggy client, and retrying cannot help. But discard is opt-in — a message someone pressed send on is never discarded by the system.
  • Typing expires on a client timer, because the sender may simply have closed their tab. A server-side timer invents a fact.
  • The focus ring is opaque in both themes — shadcn's ring-ring/50 is about 2.4:1 on white and fails WCAG 1.4.11.
