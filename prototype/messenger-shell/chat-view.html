Date header — variant A vs B

A scrolls the divider away with the rest of the transcript. B pins it to the top of the scroll area so you always know which day you are reading. The ticket asked for B.

Message actions — variant A vs C

A offers nothing; the platform context menu is the fallback. C shows a toolbar on hover. Tier 2 actions do not exist in v1, so C is showing a toolbar for actions the build does not have.

ADR behind each choice

  • Burst corners — fused reading at 4px. single narrows nothing; first narrows start-inline-end; middle and last narrow both and are therefore identical. A date divider terminates a run. Tails are structurally absent, not hidden.
  • Timestamps below the run, carrying the run's LAST message's time. A single-message run still gets one. The row is a flex container holding a text node, so justify-* does the alignment — text-align does nothing there, which cost a debugging pass.
  • "Seen" under the last OUTGOING message only. It falls out of the schema: your own sends never advance your own receipt, so your watermark can never satisfy the condition for your own message.
  • Presence is a dot, and the label says "within the last 45 seconds" — because a TTL means connected within a window, not at an instant. A green tick claims more than the system knows.
  • Unread badge and mute glyph are two marks, never merged. A badge that also mutes is a mark you cannot interpret.
  • The pending message sorts last and has no position. A client may never interpolate one from local time.
  • The scroll-follow pill is 32px and only appears when you were already at the bottom. A pill, not a bar.
  • Motion — cubic-bezier(0.14,1,0.34,1) in over 280ms, cubic-bezier(0.45,0.1,0.2,1) out over 200ms. Enter slower than exit.
  • The focus ring is opaque in both themes. shadcn's ring-ring/50 measures about 2.4:1 on white, under the 3:1 non-text minimum.
